Frequently asked questions

Is camel case reversible?

Not directly — this transformation is one-way, so the exact original text can't always be reconstructed from the output alone.

Does it handle accented letters and emoji correctly?

Yes. It encodes and decodes real UTF-8 bytes, so characters like á, ñ, €, and emoji work correctly — not just plain ASCII.

Is my text uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything runs locally in your browser in vanilla JavaScript — nothing you type ever leaves your device.
